# AI Stock Picker

Independent research and decision-support engine for short-term equity setups (2–15 trading sessions).

## Principles
- Recommendation-only: no automatic order execution.
- Separate from Pharma Radar and PSIG projects.
- Every idea must include timestamped sources, entry reference, invalidation level, targets, and risk/reward.
- No claim of predictive edge before out-of-sample backtesting and paper-trading validation.

## Initial scope
- US and European listed equities.
- Market/volume/technical screening.
- Company-news and catalyst analysis.
- Risk controls for volatility, liquidity, spreads, and gaps.
- Historical backtesting with realistic costs and slippage.

## Project status
Repository initialized. Data providers, scanner, scoring model, backtests, and Telegram reporting are not yet implemented.
